## Short description

## Full description
Bring the timeless motion of a classic Newton's cradle to your phone.

Newton's Cradle recreates the familiar chrome-and-black desk sculpture in a richly rendered study environment. Pull a ball, release it, and watch momentum travel through the cradle with synchronized impact sound and subtle haptic feedback.

Features:
- Interactive touch control
- Newton Dynamics-powered rigid-body simulation
- Synchronized single-impact clack audio
- Polished chrome cradle and atmospheric study setting
- Smooth portrait presentation
- Lightweight, focused experience

A satisfying physics desk toy for a quiet break, a quick demonstration, or the simple pleasure of watching motion transfer from one side to the other.
