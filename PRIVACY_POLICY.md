# Privacy Policy — Newton's Cradle

**Effective date:** September 14, 2026

Newton's Cradle is published by Haselton Media Group LLC.

The app does not require an account and does not ask you to provide your name, email address, contacts, precise location, photos, or files.

## Advertising and device data

The app uses Google AdMob to display banner advertising. Google and its partners may collect or process device identifiers, IP address, general location derived from IP, ad interactions, diagnostic data, and other information needed to deliver, measure, secure, and personalize advertising, subject to your consent choices and applicable law.

Where required, the app presents Google's consent message before requesting ads. If privacy options are required in your region, you can reopen them using the **Privacy choices** button in the app.

Learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## App functionality

The Newton's cradle simulation runs on your device. The app does not operate its own analytics service or transmit your cradle interactions to Haselton Media Group LLC.

## Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed specifically to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

## Data retention and deletion

Haselton Media Group LLC does not maintain user accounts or a separate database of personal information from this app. Advertising data is handled under Google's policies and retention controls.

## Security

We use established platform services and reasonable safeguards appropriate to this app. No method of electronic processing is completely secure.

## Changes

We may update this policy when the app or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published.
