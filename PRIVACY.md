# Newton's Cradle 3D Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 13, 2026

Newton's Cradle 3D is provided by Haselton Media Group LLC. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the Android app with package name `com.haseltonmediagroup.newtonscradle3d`.

## Accounts and app data

Newton's Cradle 3D does not require an account or login. The app's cradle simulation, touch input, sound, and haptic feedback are processed on your device. The app does not send simulation activity to Haselton Media Group LLC. Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK may store consent preferences locally.

## Advertising and Google services

The app displays banner and interstitial advertising using Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Google may collect or process information such as your IP address and approximate location inferred from it; advertising ID, app set ID, and other applicable device identifiers; app and advertising interactions such as app launches, ad views, and taps; and diagnostic or performance information.

Google uses advertising-related information to deliver and measure ads, provide analytics, detect fraud and invalid traffic, and maintain security. Ad personalization and identifier use depend on applicable law, your device and account settings, your choices, and Google's service configuration. Advertising data may be processed by Google and its advertising partners outside your country. Ad requests can involve data processing even when ads are not personalized.

See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and its explanation of [how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Privacy choices

The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to update consent information at launch and display a consent form when required. Ads are requested only when Google's SDK indicates that an ad request is permitted.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID through Android settings. Google also provides advertising controls through [My Ad Center](https://myadcenter.google.com/). Availability depends on your device and account.

Newton's Cradle 3D is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13.

## Retention, security, and requests

Google determines retention for information it processes under its policies. Google describes Mobile Ads SDK transmissions as encrypted using TLS. No storage or transmission method provides absolute security.

If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to provide and use it to respond to your request. Please do not send passwords or sensitive information. Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of personal information. For information controlled by Google, use Google's privacy controls or contact Google.
